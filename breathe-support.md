# Breathe with Pufi

Your daily breathing buddy. Take a short pause, follow a breathing rhythm, and help Pufi the otter grow as you build a routine.

## Contact support

Email [scofield665@gmail.com](mailto:scofield665@gmail.com?subject=Breathe%20Support) for help with Breathe, purchases or privacy. Include your app version, device model and a description of the issue. Please do not send passwords, payment card details or health records.

## Getting started

Choose a practice in Explore or start the one-minute Quick Reset from Today. Follow the visual guide at a comfortable pace. You can change sounds, voice cues and haptics in Settings. The app includes four free practices; Premium unlocks the full library and additional features.

## Restore Premium

If you previously subscribed, use Restore in the purchase screen. Use the same Apple account that made the purchase. An internet connection is required to load current subscription options and restore purchases.

## Cancel or manage a subscription

Open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, or [open subscription settings](https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions). Apple handles payments and cancellations. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. For an eligible refund, visit [Apple's Report a Problem](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/).

## Apple Health and Apple Watch

Health access is optional. Enable it in Breathe Settings and choose which Health data to share. Some insights require records already available in Apple Health and will remain empty when there is insufficient data. The Apple Watch companion offers a short breathing practice and displays Pufi's status from your paired iPhone. Widgets can show Pufi and open a quick practice.

## Safe practice

Breathe is a general wellness app, not a medical device or a treatment. Use a comfortable pace and stop if you feel dizzy or unwell. Follow each practice's safety instructions. Do not perform breath holds or intensive breathing while driving, in water or where fainting could be dangerous. Ask a qualified healthcare professional if you are unsure whether a practice is suitable for you.
