# Privacy Policy — Sugar Detector AI

Last updated: 2026-11-02

This Privacy Policy explains what data the iOS app "Sugar Detector AI" (the "App") handles, why, and who it is shared with. The App has no accounts and shows no ads.

### 1) What stays on your device
- Your scan history, photos you scanned, chat messages, sugar diary, daily limit, goal and settings are stored only on your device (and in your device backups). We do not have a copy.
- The Home Screen widget reads today's sugar total from storage shared between the App and its widget on your device.
- Trial reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device.

### 2) AI analysis (photos and questions)
When you scan a photo or ask a question, and only after you agree on the in-app consent screen:
- the photo (or the product label text from a barcode lookup), your question, the app language and the analysis mode you chose (for example "strict mode") are sent over an encrypted connection to our server and from there to our AI provider, **OpenAI**, to produce the answer;
- your health conditions are never sent: the App sends only how strict the analysis should be;
- our server does not store photos, questions or answers. It keeps only technical usage records (time, model, token counts, status) to enforce fair-use limits and costs;
- OpenAI processes this data on our behalf. Under its API terms it does not use API data to train its models and may keep it for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring, after which it is deleted.

You can decline the consent screen; nothing is sent then, and barcode lookups and the diary keep working.

### 3) Barcode lookups
When you scan a barcode, the barcode number is sent to **Open Food Facts** (openfoodfacts.org), an open food database, to get the product's ingredients and nutrition facts. No other data is sent with it.

### 4) Apple Health
If you turn on "Save to Apple Health" in Settings, the App writes the grams of sugar you mark as eaten to Apple Health (dietary sugar). The App only writes; it never reads your Health data. Health data is never sent to our server or any third party, never used for advertising, and never stored in iCloud by the App.

### 5) Purchases and device identifiers
- Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We use **RevenueCat** to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your App Store purchase receipts. See revenuecat.com/privacy.
- To protect the AI service from abuse, the App uses Apple's App Attest. Our server receives an anonymous device key and the anonymous app user ID to apply daily limits. They are not linked to your identity.

### 6) What we do not do
We do not sell data, do not track you across apps or websites, do not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and do not use your content to train models.

### 7) Children
The App is not directed at children under 13. The "Snacks for kids" mode is meant for parents checking food.

### 8) Not medical advice
The App gives general nutrition information and estimates. It is not a medical device and is not medical advice. Talk to your doctor about your diet, especially if you have diabetes.

### 9) Deleting your data
Delete the App to remove all data stored on your device. Data written to Apple Health can be deleted in the Health app. For any request, contact us.

### 10) Changes
We may update this Policy. The current version is always on this page.
