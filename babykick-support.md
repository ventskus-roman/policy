# Baby Kick — Support

For help, purchases or privacy requests, email [scofield665@gmail.com](mailto:scofield665@gmail.com?subject=Baby%20Kick%20Support). Mention Baby Kick, your app version, iOS/device version and the steps involved. Do not send passwords, Apple credentials, health notes/reports or full receipts unless a necessary secure procedure has been agreed. This page has no contact form and does not submit information automatically.

## Find your app's support identifier

If your version offers **Copy support ID** in Settings, use it and review the copied text. You may paste it into an email you choose to send. Copying does not send anything, change your analytics setting or change your subscription. The clipboard item is configured as local to this device with a five-minute expiry, and contains no health records. The identifier is pseudonymous, not a password; share it only with support. Older versions may not have this control. If it is missing or you reinstalled, tell us before trying to identify a record by email alone.

## Access or delete data

State which scope you want: local/iCloud health records, PostHog analytics, RevenueCat purchase/customer records or a support conversation. We use the identifier and a proportionate verification step to locate the right record and related aliases. Do not send health records as identity proof. No lookup or deletion is triggered automatically by this page or by copying details. For your own local records, delete individual sessions in History or clear recent contractions in the timer; this is not an erase-all action. iCloud and shared PDF copies need separate consideration.

## Restore, manage or cancel Premium

Use Restore purchases in the app with the Apple account that made the purchase. For subscriptions, [open Apple subscription settings](https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions); for eligible refund requests, use [Apple Report a Problem](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/). Removing the app or deleting a RevenueCat record does not cancel your Apple subscription. Restore can synchronize purchase records again.

## Health concerns

Baby Kick is a recording tool and does not replace medical consultation. Contact your maternity care team for concerns about movements or contractions. Support email is not an urgent medical channel.
