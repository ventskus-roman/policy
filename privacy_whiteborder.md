# White Border — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 19, 2026

White Border: Photo Frames is provided by Roman Ventskus. Contact [scofield665@gmail.com](mailto:scofield665@gmail.com) for support or privacy requests.

## Photos and local storage

White Border processes your photographs on your iPhone. We do not upload your photos, edited images, drafts or saved styles to our servers or to RevenueCat. You select photos using Apple's system photo picker. Permission to add images to your photo library is requested when you choose to save an image there. Your original photos are not modified.

The app stores its current draft, imported photo copies, preferences, saved styles and the count of free photo exports locally. Device and iCloud backups follow your Apple settings. When you share or export a photo to another app or service, you choose its destination and that service's privacy practices apply.

## Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes payments. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases, enable subscription features, restore purchases, prevent fraud and understand subscription performance. RevenueCat processes a randomly generated app user identifier, purchase and transaction history, subscription status, and technical information needed to provide its service, such as the app version, operating system, storefront and network request information. Purchase records can be associated with the same anonymous app identifier. White Border does not require an account and does not send your name or email address as customer attributes.

We do not receive your payment card details. Photos and image content are not part of subscription verification. See [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and [RevenueCat's Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/).

## Advertising and tracking

White Border has no advertising SDK, does not request your advertising identifier and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites for advertising. We do not sell your personal information. The app does not include a separate behavioral analytics or crash-reporting SDK; subscription analytics are provided by RevenueCat as described above.

## Retention and choices

You can remove photos from your working series. Deleting the app removes its local data; exported files and device backups must be managed separately. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

Apple and RevenueCat may retain purchase records for subscription operation, restoration, fraud prevention and applicable legal requirements. Contact us for requests concerning information we control. We may need enough information to locate your purchase record. Do not send passwords, payment card details or private photographs.

## Support and privacy rights

If you email support, we receive your email address and any information you include so we can respond. We and our service providers may process information outside your country. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal information and to contact your local data protection authority. Send privacy requests to [scofield665@gmail.com](mailto:scofield665@gmail.com).

## Children and updates

White Border is a general-audience photo utility, not a service directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us. We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change; the effective date identifies the current version.
