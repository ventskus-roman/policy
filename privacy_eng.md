# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

The application uses the information you provide and the pseudonymous identifiers and purchase information described below. For Keto AI Scanner, this information is processed by our server and the service providers described below to provide app functionality. It is not used for advertising or tracking.

### AI processing (Keto AI Scanner)

When you scan food or ask a follow-up question, the photo and your question are sent over an encrypted connection to our server and then to our AI service providers, OpenAI and Anthropic, solely to analyze your food and answer you. We ask for your consent in the app before the first scan. Our providers do not use this data to train their models. They may keep it for a limited time (typically up to 30 days) for abuse and safety monitoring, after which it is deleted. Your scan history (photos and results) is stored only on your device.

### Chat with the developer (NutriAI)

When you use “Chat with the developer” or “Report inaccurate estimate” in NutriAI, the text you send and any meal photo you choose to include are sent through the developer’s server to the developer in a private Telegram support group. Sending a photo is optional.

Your message includes your language, app and iOS versions, Free or Pro subscription status, and pseudonymous RevenueCat app user ID so that we can follow up on your request. An inaccurate-estimate report also includes the meal name, calorie estimate, and input method.

Support messages and replies are automatically translated using OpenAI. We use Apple Push Notification service to notify you of replies. Support messages and their associated context are stored on the developer’s server to maintain the conversation. Attached photos are held on the server until they are successfully delivered to Telegram. Messages, photos, and context are also delivered to the developer through Telegram.

You can delete the local chat history using “Delete all data” in the app. This does not automatically delete the server copy. To request deletion of the server copy, send a request in the support chat or contact scofield665@gmail.com.

### Usage analytics in NutriAI

NutriAI collects limited app activity and purchase outcomes to understand whether people can complete onboarding, save their first meal and subscribe successfully. Usage analytics is enabled by default. You can turn it off in Profile under Usage analytics. Your choice is saved across updates and local data deletion.

Events include app launches, onboarding step names, the start and successful save of a meal, analysis failure categories, paywall displays and purchase or restore outcomes. They include the app version, build and interface language. Analytics events do not include meals, photos, calorie or weight values, medical information, questionnaire answers, free text or full transaction payloads.

We use a random installation identifier and the RevenueCat app user identifier to associate activity with subscription events on our existing server. Identifiers are stored there as keyed hashes; these records are pseudonymous and can still link app activity with purchase history. Billing events come from RevenueCat. We use this analytics module for product measurement, without advertising identifiers or session recording.

App activity is retained for up to 90 days. Normalized billing records, duplicate-delivery receipts and anonymous counters are retained for up to 400 days. Minimal hashed permission and identity-link records remain until an explicit erasure request so a saved choice is respected and subscriptions can remain associated. Turning Usage analytics off clears queued app activity and removes that installation's linked analytics events and billing analytics from our server once the saved choice reaches it. The existing billing service continues to manage purchases and subscription access. For deletion requests, use the contact already listed in this policy.

### Identifiers and purchases

To manage subscriptions and enforce the free daily limit we use a pseudonymous app user ID generated by RevenueCat and a random installation ID. They are not linked to your name, email or Apple ID and are not used for advertising or tracking. Subscription purchases are processed by Apple and RevenueCat.
